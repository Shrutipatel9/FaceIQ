### Summary

The HTTP contracts are largely consistent with the requirements and stories. The main architectural flaw is `/journey`, which is owned by U5 but consumes downstream ports from U6, U7 and U11, contradicting the acyclic unit DAG (R-01). Also weigh the unspecified AnalysisResult channel, the anti-enumeration gaps on OTP resend and verify, the missing vendor contracts, and the endpoint deviation from AUTH-016.
